# Hogwarts Legacy: Demiguise Statues and Alohomora Guide

The Demiguise Moon statues are one of Hogwarts Legacy's most important collectible hunts. There are 33 Demiguise Moons hidden across Hogwarts, Hogsmeade, and the Highlands — and collecting them is the only way to upgrade Alohomora, your lock-picking spell. Without Alohomora III, dozens of locked doors, chests, and secret rooms remain inaccessible. This guide covers the Gladwin Moon questline, the full location of every Demiguise statue, and the optimal collection route.

## The Gladwin Moon Questline

### Quest: "The Caretaker's Lunar Lament"

You unlock this quest automatically after completing "The Map Chamber" main story quest and reaching Level 14. The quest giver is Gladwin Moon, the Hogwarts caretaker, who can be found near the Faculty Tower Floo Flame.

Gladwin Moon explains that he has lost his collection of Demiguise statues — small figurines of the moon-like magical creature — scattered throughout the castle and surrounding areas. The catch: Demiguise statues are invisible during the day. They only reveal themselves at night. This mechanic is central to the entire collectible hunt.

### Alohomora I: The First 9 Statues

Gladwin Moon requires 9 Demiguise Moons to teach you Alohomora I. These are collected exclusively within Hogwarts Castle (the first batch you find during the quest's guided section).

**Quest Progression**:
1. Speak to Gladwin Moon near the Faculty Tower.
2. He gives you a hint about the Hospital Wing. Travel there (Floo Flame: Hospital Wing).
3. Find the first Demiguise statue in the Hospital Wing. Nighttime only — if it is daytime, open the map and press F/Right Stick to "Wait" until night.
4. The statue glows with a blue-white shimmer. Approach and collect it.
5. Return to Gladwin Moon. He mentions the Prefects' Bathroom and the Library Restricted Section.
6. Collect additional statues as prompted. The quest marks 3–4 guided locations.
7. Once you have collected your first 9 statues (including the guided ones and any you find independently), return to Gladwin Moon.
8. He teaches you **Alohomora I**, which opens Level 1 locks.

### Alohomora II: The Next 13 Statues

After learning Alohomora I, Gladwin Moon tells you that more Demiguise Moons are waiting to be found. He requires **13 additional statues** (22 total) for Alohomora II.

This requires venturing beyond Hogwarts into Hogsmeade and the initial Highlands areas. Level 1 locks throughout the castle now become accessible, and many of them contain additional Demiguise statues — creating a satisfying unlock loop.

Return to Gladwin Moon with 22 total statues collected. He teaches you **Alohomora II**, which opens Level 2 locks.

### Alohomora III: All 33 Statues

The final upgrade requires collecting all **33 Demiguise Moons**. This includes the most remote Highlands locations, often inside locked houses in small hamlets. Level 2 locks are now accessible, and they contain the final statues.

Return to Gladwin Moon with all 33 statues. He teaches you **Alohomora III**, which opens Level 3 locks — the highest lock level in the game. Every locked door, chest, and secret room in Hogwarts Legacy is now accessible.

## Complete Demiguise Moon Location List

### Hogwarts Castle (10 Statues)

1. **Hospital Wing**: On a shelf to the left of the main door. The first statue you encounter during the quest.
2. **Library Restricted Section**: Descend the stairs. In the back corner on a table.
3. **Prefects' Bathroom**: Level 1 lock on the door (Bathroom is on the fifth floor, near the Gryffindor common room area). The statue is on a bench inside.
4. **Faculty Tower**: Near the Floo Flame. On a small table in the corridor.
5. **Great Hall**: Behind the staff table, in the small storage alcove on the right side of the hall. On a barrel.
6. **Bell Tower Wing — Music Room**: Level 1 lock door. Inside the music room, the statue is on a desk.
7. **Long Gallery**: The hallway connecting the Bell Tower to the Grand Staircase. On a table midway down the gallery.
8. **Defence Against the Dark Arts Tower**: Inside Professor Hecat's classroom, on her desk. Enter via the main DADA tower entrance.
9. **Muggle Studies Classroom**: Level 1 lock. The statue is on a bookshelf in the back.
10. **Headmaster's Office**: Requires Alohomora III and the key from the "Niamh Fitzgerald's Trial" story progress. The statue is on a side table near the Pensieve.

### Hogsmeade (13 Statues)

11. **Tomes and Scrolls**: Inside the shop behind the counter. On a shelf.
12. **Dogweed and Deathcap**: Inside the greenhouse shop. On a crate near the back.
13. **The Three Broomsticks — Upper Floor**: Level 1 lock on the door at the top of the stairs. The statue is in the private room on a table.
14. **Honeydukes — Back Room**: Level 1 lock. Enter Honeydukes, go through the back door. Statue on a crate.
15. **Gladrags Wizardwear**: Inside the shop, on a display table.
16. **Dervish and Banges**: Inside the shop, behind the counter on a shelf.
17. **The Magic Neep**: Inside the vegetable shop, on a barrel near the back.
18. **Zonko's Joke Shop**: On a shelf behind the counter.
19. **J. Pippin's Potions**: Inside the potion shop, on a table to the left.
20. **Hogsmeade — Small House (East)**: A small house on the eastern edge of Hogsmeade. Level 1 lock. Statue inside on a nightstand.
21. **Hogsmeade — Small House (North)**: Near the northern Floo Flame entrance. Level 1 lock. Statue on a table.
22. **Hogsmeade — Abandoned Shop**: The haunted shop from the "Minding Your Own Business" quest (PlayStation exclusive). Statue on the upper floor.
23. **Hogsmeade — The Old Fool Pub**: Level 1 lock on the upstairs room. Statue on a table near the window.

### Highlands (10 Statues)

24. **Upper Hogsfield**: Inside the small house on the northern edge of the hamlet. Level 1 lock. Statue on a window sill.
25. **Lower Hogsfield**: Inside the house near the eastern edge. Level 1 lock. Statue on a table.
26. **Aranshire**: Inside the house closest to the Floo Flame. Level 1 lock. Statue on a bookshelf.
27. **Feldcroft**: Inside the house on the northern slope (near the Rookwood Castle path). Level 2 lock. Statue on a table.
28. **Keenbridge**: The hamlet in the southern Highlands. Inside the house with a Level 1 lock. Statue on a barrel.
29. **Bainburgh**: Western hamlet. Inside a house with Level 1 lock. Statue on a nightstand.
30. **Pitt-upon-Ford**: Northern hamlet. Inside a house with Level 1 lock. Statue on a shelf.
31. **Marunweem**: Eastern hamlet near the coast. Level 2 lock on the house. Statue near the fireplace.
32. **Cragcroft**: Southern coastal hamlet. Level 2 lock on the house. Statue on a table.
33. **Brocburrow**: Inside the house with a Level 1 lock. Statue on a crate near the entrance.

## Optimal Collection Route

Since Demiguise Moons are only visible at night, your collection pattern should be:

1. **Fast travel** to the nearest Floo Flame of each location.
2. **Open the map** and press F/Right Stick to Wait until night.
3. **Collect all statues** in that area before moving on.
4. Repeat for the next area.

### Phase 1: Hogwarts Only (9 statues)
- Collect all 10 Hogwarts statues (you only need 9 for Alohomora I).
- Hospital Wing → Library Restricted Section → Faculty Tower → Great Hall → Bell Tower Wing → Long Gallery → DADA Tower.
- Return to Gladwin Moon for Alohomora I.

### Phase 2: Hogsmeade + Accessible Highlands (13 statues)
- Hogsmeade: All 13 statues. Each shop is open during the day, but statues are invisible. Wait until night.
- Upper Hogsfield, Lower Hogsfield, Aranshire, Keenbridge (Level 1 locks accessible with Alohomora I).
- Return to Gladwin Moon at 22 total for Alohomora II.

### Phase 3: Remaining Highlands + Locked Hogwarts (11 statues)
- Feldcroft, Bainburgh, Pitt-upon-Ford, Marunweem, Cragcroft, Brocburrow.
- Headmaster's Office (requires Alohomora III and story progress).
- Muggle Studies Classroom (Level 1 lock, but hidden behind a secondary puzzle — use Revelio).
- Return to Gladwin Moon at 33 total for Alohomora III.

## Alohomora Lock-Picking Mini-Game

The lock-picking mechanic in Hogwarts Legacy is a simple two-step process:

1. Rotate the green sparks to align with the central mechanism. This unlocks the red tumbler.
2. Rotate the red sparks to align with the central mechanism. This opens the lock.

**Tips**:
- Level 1 locks: 2–3 tumblers. Takes 5–10 seconds.
- Level 2 locks: 4–5 tumblers. Takes 15–20 seconds.
- Level 3 locks: 6–7 tumblers. Takes 20–30 seconds.
- The game auto-advances to the next tumbler when you align correctly. No penalty for failure — you can retry indefinitely.
- With Alohomora III, you can set the lock to auto-solve (Story Mode difficulty option only). This is a quality-of-life feature for players who find the mini-game tedious after dozens of locks.

## What Alohomora III Unlocks

With fully upgraded Alohomora, you gain access to:

- **Hogwarts Collection Chests**: Roughly 30+ chests behind Level 2–3 locks containing gear, wand handles, and cosmetic items.
- **Demiguise Statues**: The 10 final statues in locked areas (Headmaster's Office, certain classrooms).
- **Field Guide Pages**: Many pages are inside locked rooms — the Revelio Pages in particular.
- **Hogsmeade Shops**: Back rooms, upstairs areas, and private quarters of shopkeepers — containing gear and gold.
- **Highlands Hamlets**: Every locked house in every hamlet — containing gear chests, gold, and occasionally rare potion recipes.
- **The Headmaster's Office**: The crowning locked area. Contains a legendary chest, field guide pages, and significant lore.

## Tips and Tricks

- **Constantly cast Revelio** inside locked rooms. Many have hidden wall panels or additional puzzle mechanisms.
- **Demiguise statues chime** when you are near them. Listen for a distinct tinkling sound — it is a different audio cue than Revelio pages or treasure chests.
- **If you miss the Sebastian quest window**: Some locked areas tied to companion quests remain inaccessible if you turn Sebastian in. The Undercroft and certain Scriptorium areas, for example.
- **Alohomora works on chests too** — not just doors. Look for glowing locks on treasure chests throughout the game.
- **Completion tracking**: Open the Collections menu → Field Guide → scroll to the Demiguise Statues tab for a counter showing collected/total. Hovering shows per-region progress.
- **Gladwin Moon gives additional dialogue** after each Alohomora upgrade, with slightly unhinged stories about his fear of Demiguises. These are purely for entertainment.
