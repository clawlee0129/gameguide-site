## Introduction

Natlan, the Nation of Pyro and the sixth major region in Genshin Impact, released in Version 5.0 with a fundamentally different exploration paradigm. Unlike previous nations where exploration was limited by stamina and Elemental Sight, Natlan introduces Saurian Indwelling—the ability to possess dragon-like creatures native to the region. Each of Natlan's six tribes has a unique Saurian species that enables specific traversal mechanics: rock climbing, water surfing, grappling hook traversal, high-speed ground sprints, aerial flight, and underwater currents.

Natlan's combat introduces the Night Soul system—a unique energy mechanic for Natlan characters that replaces standard Energy partially with Phlogiston-based state management. The Tablet of Tona replaces the traditional Offering System, and Reputation is spread across six individual tribes rather than a single regional commission board.

This guide covers everything: how to unlock Natlan (requirements and optimal path), the complete Saurian Indwelling system with all six forms, Night Soul mechanics for the full Natlan character roster, World Boss locations (including post-5.0 additions), and the Collective of Plenty reputation system.

## How to Unlock Natlan

### Prerequisites

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Adventure Rank | AR 28+ (minimum) |
| Archon Quest | Complete Chapter IV: Act VI — "Bedtime Story" (Fontaine finale) |
| World Quest | Automatically triggers "Natlan! A New Adventure!" upon approaching the region |
| Statue of the Seven | Unlock any Natlan Statue of the Seven to reveal the map |
| Teleport Waypoints | Automatically unlocked near the entrance from Sumeru's Desert of Hadramaveth |

### Fastest Route to Natlan

1. Complete the Fontaine Archon Quest through Act V ("Masquerade of the Guilty"). Act VI ("Bedtime Story") is a short epilogue that officially grants you passage.
2. Teleport to the Statue of the Seven nearest to Sumeru's Desert of Hadramaveth — the waypoint at Bayt Al-Sukkar or the northernmost Desert waypoint.
3. Head west past the sandstorm barrier. A cutscene triggers when you approach the Natlan border, opening the path.
4. Unlock the first Natlan Statue of the Seven to sync Pyro Traveler and reveal the region map.
5. Unlock all visible Teleport Waypoints — there are 8 waypoints on the initial map segment (Version 5.0 release area).

### What Level Should You Be?

At AR 28, Natlan enemies scale to approximately Level 55-65 (World Level 3-4). The region is designed to be playable immediately after Fontaine without significant grinding. World Bosses (Level 72-93 depending on World Level) are manageable with a level 70-80 Fontaine team.

**Recommended**: AR 35+ with at least one Level 70 DPS before attempting the Natlan Archon Quest's boss sequence. The 5.0 finale involves a significantly longer combat gauntlet than previous Archon Quests.

## The Saurian Indwelling System

Saurian Indwelling allows you to possess ("indwell") a Saurian creature by interacting with a glowing orange Saurian relic found throughout Natlan. While indwelling, you control the Saurian directly and gain its movement abilities. Each of the six Natlan tribes has one Saurian species.

### How to Indwell

1. Find a Saurian Relic — glowing orange stone formations scattered across the region (marked on the minimap with a claw icon).
2. Approach and press the interact key (F on keyboard, corresponding controller button).
3. Your character "transforms" — you now control the Saurian. Your HP, Phlogiston gauge, and abilities change to the Saurian's.
4. To exit, press the indwell key again or the Saurian will automatically exit when:
   - Phlogiston reaches zero
   - You come into contact with deep water (most Saurians can't swim)
   - You press the exit key

### Phlogiston — The Saurian Energy Gauge

All Saurian abilities consume **Phlogiston**, represented by an orange bar below the Saurian's HP.

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Maximum | Varies by Saurian type (100-150 base). Increases with Tablet of Tona level. |
| While indwelling | Passively drains at ~1 unit per second (ambient consumption). Abilities drain 10-30 units each. |
| Recharging | Collect Phlogiston particles from orange vents, crystal formations, and defeated Natlan enemies. Saurian relics automatically grant full Phlogiston on indwell. |
| Depleted | Saurian automatically exits. You must collect Phlogiston and indwell again. |

## Night Soul System — Natlan Characters

All characters from Natlan have a unique **Night Soul** (NS) mechanic instead of the standard Elemental Burst energy system. Night Soul replaces traditional Energy Recharge with Phlogiston-based state management.

### Night Soul Fundamentals

| Property | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Night Soul Gauge | Appears as an orange bar alongside HP. Max varies per character (50-120 NS points). |
| Gaining NS | Natlan characters gain Night Soul from their Elemental Skills, normal attacks during Blessing state, and collecting Phlogiston particles. |
| Blessing State | When a Natlan character reaches 100% NS gauge, they enter "Blessing State" — enhanced attacks, increased damage, and alternate skill effects. |
| Depletion | NS passively drains during Blessing state (~8-12 NS/sec). When depleted, the character exits Blessing state. |
| Burst Activation | Natlan characters do not use Elemental Burst charges. Instead, their Burst costs a flat amount of NS (typically 40-60) and has no cooldown (only limited by NS availability). |

### Natlan Character Night Soul Mechanics

| Character | NS Max | Blessing Effect | NS Burst Cost | Key Mechanic |
|-----------|--------|----------------|---------------|-------------|
| **Mavuika** (5★ Pyro Archon, Claymore) | 120 | All attacks become Pyro-infused. Charged Attacks become Sun Wheel (spinning disc). | 50 | Sun Wheel orbit damage. C2 increases Phlogiston regeneration. |
| **Kinich** (5★ Dendro, Claymore) | 100 | Normal Attacks fire Dendro projectiles. Scales with ATK. | 45 | Ranged Dendro DPS with AoE. Blessing state gives infinite Dendro application. |
| **Mualani** (5★ Hydro, Catalyst) | 100 | Surfs on a shark-shaped wave. Normal Attacks become Hydro-infused surf attacks. Move speed +80%. | 50 | Unique Hydro catalyst with traversal integration. |

| **Chasca** (5★ Anemo, Bow) | 90 | Charged Attacks become Anemo-infused piercing arrows. AoE suction on hit. | 40 | Anemo DPS with crowd control. |

| **Ororon** (4★ Electro, Bow) | 75 | Charged Shots become Electro-infused AoE. Increased arrow velocity. | 30 | Ranged Electro DPS. Fast charged shots. |

### Night Soul Team Synergy

All Natlan characters in the party contribute to a shared "Collective of Plenty" passive: each Natlan character in the party increases Phlogiston regeneration rate by 10% (max 40% with 4 Natlan characters). Additionally, when any Natlan character exits Blessing state, all Natlan party members gain 15 Night Soul. This enables rapid rotation cycling — Character A enters Blessing, depletes NS, exits → others gain 15 NS → Character B enters Blessing, and so on.

## Tablet of Tona — Natlan's Offering System

The Tablet of Tona is located at the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, Natlan's central hub. It accepts Pyro Sigils (obtained from chests, quests, and exploration in Natlan).

### Rewards (Ongoing — Version 5.x)

| Level Range | Pyro Sigils per Level | Key Rewards |
|------------|----------------------|-------------|
| 1-10 | 30 each | Primogems, Intertwined Fates, Pyroculus Resonance Stones |
| 11-20 | 30 each | Talent Books (Natlan series), Character EXP |
| 21-30 | 30 each | Mora, Weapon Ascension Materials, Billets |
| 31-40 | 30 each | Crown of Insight (at 40), Natlan Namecard, additional Saurian Phlogiston capacity |
| 41-50 | (Pending map expansions beyond 5.x) | Additional rewards TBD |

**Note**: The Tablet of Tona's maximum level increases with each Natlan map expansion. As of Version 5.x, the cap is expected to reach 50 after the final Natlan area release (similar to Sacred Sakura, Tree of Dreams, and Fountain of Lucine).

### Phlogiston Capacity Upgrades

At specific Tablet levels, your Saurian Phlogiston gauge increases permanently:
- Level 5: +15 Phlogiston capacity
- Level 15: +15 Phlogiston capacity
- Level 25: +20 Phlogiston capacity
- Level 35: +20 Phlogiston capacity

Maximum Phlogiston with fully upgraded Tablet: ~220 (varies by Saurian type).

## Collective of Plenty Reputation

Unlike previous regions where Reputation was managed by a single NPC, Natlan distributes Reputation across the six tribes. Each tribe has an Obsidian Ring that tracks your standing.

### Tribe Reputation Levels

| Level | EXP Required (per tribe) | Cumulative Rewards |
|-------|-------------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | 200 | Tribe Namecard background, 50 Primogems |
| 2 | 400 | Saurian Traversal Upgrade (faster speed, lower Phlogiston cost for that tribe's Saurian) |
| 3 | 600 | Pyro Treasure Compass Blueprint (unlocked at any tribe Level 4) |
| 4 | 800 | Tribe-exclusive Weapon Blueprint, 100 Primogems |
| 5 | 1,000 | Saurian Combat Upgrade (higher damage + new ability for that tribe's Saurian) |
| 6 | 1,200 | Tribe Namecard (equipped), 150 Primogems, Special Dish Recipe |

### How to Gain Reputation EXP

| Activity | EXP | Frequency |
|----------|-----|-----------|
| Tribe Bounties | 60-100 | 3 per week (shared across all tribes) |
| Tribe Requests | 40-80 | 3 per week |
| World Quests (per tribe) | 100-300 | One-time |
| Explore tribal territory (chest %) | 20 per 10% exploration milestone | One-time per milestone |
| Complete Tribe-specific Challenges | 50-100 | One-time per challenge |

### Weekly Cap

You can complete 3 Bounties and 3 Requests per week total across all six tribes. Focus on one or two tribes at a time rather than spreading Reputation EXP thinly.

## Exploration Tips & Hidden Mechanics

### Phlogiston Hotspots

Thermal vents (marked with orange glow on the ground) provide infinite Phlogiston regeneration while standing on them. These are placed strategically near Saurian Relics and challenge areas. If you're running out of Phlogiston mid-indwell, look for the nearest thermal vent.

### Saurian Relic Fast Travel

Activated Saurian Relics (ones you've already interacted with) appear on the map and can be teleported to directly. Use them as waypoints — they're often placed near Pyroculi, chest clusters, and boss arenas.

### Combat While Indwelling

You do not need to exit Saurian form to fight. All Saurians have combat abilities (Normal Attack, Charged Skill, Plunge). The Tatankasaur has the highest Saurian combat damage, followed by Qucusaur and Tepetlisaur. Saurian combat is viable for overworld enemies but not recommended for World Bosses or Domains — Saurian damage does not scale with your character's stats.

### Achievement-Based Exploration

Natlan has 3 commission series tied to each tribe, each yielding 20-35 Primogems in achievements. Set your Daily Commission region to Natlan as soon as it's available to start progress on these.

### Secret Tribe-Owned Areas

Each tribe territory contains one "sacred cave" or "proving ground" that only opens after reaching Reputation Level 4 with that tribe. These contain Luxurious Chests (40 Primogems each), a Tribe-exclusive 4-star weapon, and Phlogiston capacity upgrades.
