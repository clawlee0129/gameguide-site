## Introduction

Genshin Impact is a massive game with dozens of interlocking systems that aren't always explained well—or at all—in-game. New players face a barrage of information: Resin, Primogems, Wishes, Battle Pass, Artifacts, Constellations, Pity systems. Making the wrong decisions early can set you back weeks of progress or waste precious Primogems that could have secured a 5-star character.

This guide explains every core system a new player needs to understand in their first 30 Adventure Ranks. It also catalogs the most common beginner mistakes—the ones that veteran players collectively spent millions of Primogems learning the hard way—so you don't repeat them.

## The Resin System: Your Daily Energy Budget

Original Resin is the time-gated stamina system that governs almost all meaningful progression. Managing it efficiently is the single most important habit for long-term account strength.

### Resin Basics

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Maximum capacity | 200 |
| Regeneration rate | 1 Resin per 8 minutes (7.5 per hour, 180 per day) |
| Time to fill from 0 | 26 hours 40 minutes |
| Overflow storage | None — Resin stops regenerating at 200/200 |

### What Resin Is Spent On

| Activity | Resin Cost | AR Requirement | Notes |
|----------|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Ley Line: Blossom of Revelation (Character EXP) | 20 | AR 8 | Blue circles on map |
| Ley Line: Blossom of Wealth (Mora) | 20 | AR 12 | Gold circles on map |
| World Bosses (Elite) | 40 | AR 10+ | 1 run. Claim with Resin after defeating. |
| Weekly Trounce Domain (Boss) | 30 (first 3), 60 (subsequent) | Varies | 3 discounted claims per week |
| Artifact Domain | 20 (or 1 Condensed Resin) | AR 25+ | — |
| Talent Book Domain | 20 (or 1 Condensed Resin) | AR 27+ | Domain open specific days of the week |
| Weapon Ascension Domain | 20 (or 1 Condensed Resin) | AR 20+ | Domain open specific days |

### Condensed Resin

Unlocked at Liyue Reputation Level 3. Craft 1 Condensed Resin at any Alchemy Bench using:
- 40 Original Resin
- 1 Crystal Core (captured from Crystalflies)

Condensed Resin can be spent on Artifact Domains, Talent Domains, Weapon Domains, and Ley Lines to claim **double rewards** in a single run. This saves time and effectively doubles your domain efficiency.

**Always keep your Condensed Resin below the cap of 5.** Craft Condensed Resin before logging off if your Resin is above 160. This way your natural regeneration doesn't cap out overnight.

### Resin Allocation Priority (AR 1-45)

| AR Range | Resin Priority |
|----------|---------------|
| 1-25 | World Bosses (character ascension materials) + Ley Lines (EXP/Mora) |
| 25-44 | World Bosses + Weapon Ascension Domains + Weekly Bosses |
| 45+ | Artifact Domains (unlocks guaranteed 5-star per run) |

**Golden Rule**: Do not farm Artifact Domains before AR 45. At AR 45, domains guarantee at least one 5-star artifact per 20-Resin run. Before AR 45, the drop rate is significantly lower—you'll waste Resin on 4-star artifacts that you'll replace immediately. Use the free Gladiator's Finale and Wanderer's Troupe pieces you get from bosses until AR 45.

## The Gacha System & Pity Mechanics

Genshin Impact's gacha is built on a "pity" system that guarantees 5-star drops after a certain number of pulls.

### Wish Fundamentals

| Currency | Purpose | Source |
|----------|---------|--------|
| Primogems | Converted to Intertwined/Acquaint Fates | Quests, exploration, events, Battle Pass, Welkin, direct purchase |
| Intertwined Fate | Limited character/weapon banners | 160 Primogems each. **Only spend on these.** |
| Acquaint Fate | Standard banner (Wanderlust Invocation) | Free from Battle Pass, AR rewards, character ascensions. **Never buy with Primogems.** |

### Pity System (Character Event Wish)

| Pull # | Guarantee |
|--------|----------|
| 1-73 | Base 0.6% 5-star rate. "Soft pity" at pull 74 — rate increases linearly to 100% at pull 90. |
| 74-89 | Soft pity zone. Most 5-stars are pulled between 74-80. |
| 90 | **Hard pity**. Guaranteed 5-star if none before. |
| 1-90 (50/50) | When you pull a 5-star, 50% chance it's the featured character, 50% chance it's a Standard 5-star (Diluc, Jean, Mona, Keqing, Qiqi, Tighnari, Dehya). |
| After losing 50/50 | Next 5-star is **guaranteed** the featured character. This guarantee **carries over** to future banners. |

### Weapon Banner (Epitomized Path)

| Pull # | Guarantee |
|--------|----------|
| 1-62 | Base rate 0.7%. Soft pity starts at 63. |
| 80 | Hard pity. |
| 50/50 at 5-star | 75% chance one of the two featured weapons, 25% chance Standard weapon. |
| Epitomized Path | Set a target weapon. If you get two 5-star weapons that aren't your target, the third 5-star is guaranteed to be your selected weapon. **Path points DO NOT carry over between banners.** |

**Weapon Banner Rule**: Only pull if you can afford 210+ Fates (hard pity ×3 worst case). The Epitomized Path resets every banner—pulling without enough to guarantee your target is gambling.

### F2P Primogem Income (Monthly Estimate)

| Source | Monthly Primogems |
|--------|------------------|
| Daily Commissions (60/day) | 1,800 |
| Spiral Abyss (Floors 9-12, 36★) | 1,200 (×2 per month) |
| Events (flagship + minor) | ~1,200-1,800 |
| Livestream Codes (×3) | 300 |
| Paimon's Bargains (Stardust exchange) | 5 Intertwined + 5 Acquaint (equivalent to 1,600) |
| Battle Pass (free track) | 5 Acquaint Fates (equivalent to 800) |
| Welkin Moon (optional, $5) | 3,000 |

**F2P Total**: ~7,000-8,000 Primogems/month (~44-50 Wishes/month).

**With Welkin**: ~10,000-11,000 Primogems/month (~62-68 Wishes/month).

At F2P rates, you can guarantee a featured 5-star every ~3 months (2 full patches). With Welkin, every ~2 months.

## Battle Pass: Is It Worth It?

The Battle Pass (BP) has a free track and a paid "Gnostic Hymn" track ($10). You have ~6 weeks per BP period.

### Free Track (Always Worth Grabbing)

| BP Level | Free Reward |
|----------|------------|
| 5, 15, 25, 35, 45 | Acquaint Fate |
| 50 | 1 of 5 4-star weapons (The Black Sword, Serpent Spine, Deathmatch, Solar Pearl, The Viridescent Hunt) — **only on paid track** |

### Paid Track ($10 Gnostic Hymn)

| Category | What You Get (Total at BP 50) |
|----------|------------------------------|
| Intertwined Fates | 4 |
| Primogems | 680 |
| Weapon | 1 of 5 exclusive BP weapons |
| Character EXP | 162 Hero's Wit equivalents |
| Mora | 2,880,000 |
| Talent Books | ~120 total (random region) |
| Mystic Enhancement Ore | 384 |

### Gnostic Hymn Value Assessment

The $10 BP provides resources equivalent to approximately 1,500-2,000 Resin in character EXP and Mora alone—about 8-10 days of natural Resin regeneration. For new players, the BP weapons are competitive 4-star options that compete with gacha 4-stars:
- **Serpent Spine**: Top-tier Claymore, BiS 4-star for many characters
- **Deathmatch**: Strong Polearm with Crit Rate substat
- **The Black Sword**: Solid Sword option
- **Solar Pearl**: Good Catalyst for on-field DPS

**Verdict**: Worth buying if you're actively building 2-4 characters. The weapon alone is worth ~20-30 Wishes (gacha 4-star weapon), and the resource savings are significant for accounts under AR 50. Not worth buying if you're only maintaining existing characters.

## Daily Commission Optimization

Daily Commissions are your primary source of Primogems (60/day) and Adventure EXP. Starting at AR 12, you receive 4 commissions per day from the Adventurers' Guild.

### Efficient Commission Strategy

| Tip | Why |
|-----|-----|
| Set commission region to the newest nation | New region commissions have more achievements tied to specific repeatable quest outcomes |
| Clear all 4 commissions | Only then can you claim the daily bonus from Katheryne (additional Primogems + EXP) |
| Use Condensed Resin for Ley Lines if commission is "Defeat X enemies" | Minimizes travel time |
| Attack enemy commission groups with AoE | Faster than single-target setups |
| Skip NPC conversation commissions if you've completed their achievements | Some NPC commissions take 2-3 minutes of dialogue. Skip them (use Encounter Points from exploration instead) |

### Encounter Points System (Version 4.1+)

As you explore and open chests, you accumulate Encounter Points. You can use Encounter Points to claim daily commission rewards **without doing the commissions**—spending the points instead. This lets you skip long NPC quest commissions and just do the fast combat ones. You can see your Encounter Points on the Adventurer Handbook page.

## Mistakes to Avoid — The "Don't Do This" List

### 1. Spending Primogems on the Standard Banner

Never, under any circumstance, convert Primogems to Acquaint Fates. Standard banner 5-stars appear naturally as you play (from Battle Pass Fates, character ascension rewards, and losing 50/50s on limited banners). The Standard banner has no limited characters, no rate-up, and no guarantee for specific characters or weapons. Every Acquaint Fate you buy with Primogems is a 5-star you'll eventually get for free.

### 2. Spending Fragile Resin Before AR 45

Fragile Resin instantly restores 60 Original Resin. The game gives you ~40 Fragile Resin from AR rewards and the Adventurer Handbook. At AR 45, Artifact Domains guarantee 5-star drops. Using Fragile Resin before AR 45 means you're effectively trading future 5-star artifacts for 4-star garbage. Hoard them all until AR 45.

**Exception**: Using 2-3 Fragile Resin to ascend a character whose World Boss won't respawn until next week (Weekly Boss materials) is acceptable. Don't hoard to the point of blocking progression.

### 3. Leveling Every Character Equally

Resources (Mora, Hero's Wit, Talent Books, Ascension Materials) are finite. By AR 50, a single character costs ~7 million Mora and ~400 Hero's Wit to fully max (Level 90, Talents 9/9/9). Spreading resources across 8+ characters early means all of them are underleveled and none can carry you through content.

**Focus on 4 characters**: 1 main DPS → their sub-DPS → support → healer/shielder. Once the core 4 can clear content comfortably, expand to a second team for Spiral Abyss.

### 4. Farming Artifacts for Perfect Substats Before Building Talents/Weapons

Talents and weapons provide guaranteed, deterministic damage increases. Artifacts are RNG. A character with 90/90, talents 8/8/8, and mediocre (correct main stats only) artifacts will out-damage a character with 70/80, talents 4/4/4, and "god-roll" artifacts. Prioritize guaranteed upgrades first.

### 5. Ignoring Elemental Mastery on Reaction Teams

On teams that rely on Vaporize, Melt, Hyperbloom, Burgeon, or Quickbloom, Elemental Mastery is often more valuable than ATK%. A Hyperbloom trigger (Kuki Shinobu or Raiden) with 900 EM will deal 4-5× more Hyperbloom damage than one with 100 EM. For reaction triggers, EM main stat artifacts are not a cop-out—they're optimal.

### 6. Burning Starglitter on Materials

Masterless Starglitter (from 4-star duplicate pulls) and Masterless Stardust (from 3-star weapons) are best used for Intertwined Fates and specific 4-star characters in Paimon's Bargains. Buying Mora, EXP books, or Ascension materials from the shop with Starglitter is a permanent loss—you can farm those resources with Resin; you can't farm Fates.

### 7. Skyward Spine vs The Catch — Wasting a Billet

If you pull Skyward Spine from the Standard banner, do not refine it. It's mediocre on most characters but functional as a stat stick. The Catch R5 (free from Inazuma Fishing Association) outperforms it on Raiden Shogun by ~4-5%, and most other Polearm characters have better options. Do not use Billets on Crescent Pike or Prototype Starglitter unless you're absolutely desperate.

### 8. Unlocking Story Quests and Not Completing Them

When you unlock a Story Quest (using Story Keys from Daily Commissions), it locks the characters involved in that quest until you complete it. If you unlock 5 Story Quests and don't finish them, you'll be blocked from starting new quests, event quests, or co-op with characters that appear in those quests. Unlock quests only when you intend to complete them.

### 9. Ignoring Weekly Bosses After AR 45+

Weekly Bosses at Level 90 drop Billets (Northlander/Central Laboratory/Midlander) and Dream Solvents (which convert Weekly Boss materials). These are time-gated—you can only claim 3 discounted runs per week. Even if you don't need the boss materials now, stockpiling Billets and Solvents prevents future bottlenecks when a new character needs a weapon or boss material conversion.

### 10. Converting Genesis Crystals to Primogems Without Checking Outfits

Genesis Crystals (premium currency, direct purchase) can buy character outfits (1,680 Crystals, ~$15-20 during the patch they release; ~$25 after). If you convert Genesis Crystals to Primogems, you can't convert back. Check if any outfits interest you before converting.

## New Player Roadmap (AR 1-50)

| AR Range | Goal | Key Activities |
|----------|------|---------------|
| 1-15 | Mondstadt Archon Quest Prologue | Main story progression. Unlock all Mondstadt waypoints. |
| 15-25 | Liyue Archon Quest Chapter I | Complete Liyue story. Start collecting Geoculi. |
| 25-30 | Ascension Quest 1 | Clear the Ascension Domain (AR 25 cap). Unlock Inazuma Archon Quest prerequisites. |
| 30-35 | AR 30+ World Level increase | Ascend main DPS to 60/70. Start farming talent books for core 4-5 characters. |
| 35-40 | Ascension Quest 2 | AR 35 cap. Ascend characters to 70/80. Start Weekly Boss farming (3x/week). |
| 40-44 | Save Fragile Resin | Focus on guaranteed upgrades: weapons to 80/90, talents to 6/6/6+ for main DPS. |
| 45 | Artifact Farming Begins | Co-op Artifact Domains for efficiency. Target: correct main stats on 5-star pieces. |
| 45-50 | Teambuilding | Build second team for Spiral Abyss. Ascend supports to 70/80 (A4 passive unlock). |
| 50+ | Endgame Optimization | Fine-tune artifacts for 2+ teams. Abyss Floor 12 pushes. Prep for new character releases. |
