## Introduction

Raiden Shogun, the Electro Archon and ruler of Inazuma, is one of Genshin Impact's most versatile characters. While her National variant (Raiden / Xiangling / Xingqiu / Bennett) is a top-tier balanced comp, her Hypercarry team elevates Raiden from driver to primary damage dealer, concentrating the entire team's buffs into her 7-second Musou Shinsetsu window. At C0 with The Catch R5, Raiden Hypercarry clears Floor 12 consistently. At C2 (ignoring 60% of enemy DEF during Musou Shinsetsu), she becomes a top-3 speedrun character.

This guide covers the complete Hypercarry build: C0 vs C2 damage comparison, weapon rankings for both F2P and premium options, 4pc Emblem optimization (including the critical Energy Recharge breakpoints), the premium team composition (Raiden / Kujou Sara C6 / Kazuha / Bennett), and the precise rotation that maximizes her 7-second Musou Shinsetsu window.

## Raiden Shogun Kit Mechanics

- **Elemental Skill (Eye of Stormy Judgment)**: Deploys an eye that follows the active character. Deals coordinated Electro attacks every 0.9 seconds when the active character deals damage. Also buffs party members' Elemental Burst DMG by 0.3% per point of Energy Cost (e.g., 24% for an 80-cost burst at Talent 10). Duration: 25 seconds. Cooldown: 10 seconds.
- **Elemental Burst (Secret Art: Musou Shinsetsu)**: 90 Energy Cost. Raiden enters Musou Shinsetsu state for 7 seconds. All attacks become Electro-infused with separate multipliers (not Normal Attack talent). During this state, her attacks regenerate Energy for the entire party (2.5 flat Energy, 5 times max). Damage scales with Resolve stacks (gained from party members using their Elemental Bursts).
- **A4 Passive (Enlightened One)**: Raiden gains 0.4% Electro DMG Bonus for every 1% Energy Recharge above 100%. At 270% ER, this provides 68% Electro DMG Bonus — functionally a free Electro Goblet.

## C0 vs C2: The Constellation Gap

### C2: Steelbreaker

> While in Musou Shinsetsu state, Raiden's attacks ignore 60% of enemy DEF.

This is the single largest Constellation power spike for any limited 5-star character. Here's why:

**DEF Calculation**: Enemy DEF reduces incoming damage by the formula `DEF / (DEF + 5 × Character Level + 500)`. Against a Level 100 enemy, your Level 90 character's attacks are reduced by approximately 50%.

Ignoring 60% of that DEF means:
- Without C2: Enemy effective DEF = 100% → ~50% damage reduction
- With C2: Enemy effective DEF = 40% → ~28% damage reduction
- Net damage increase: (1 - 0.28) / (1 - 0.50) = 1.44 → **~44% damage increase**

| Comparison | C0 Raiden | C2 Raiden |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Musou Shinsetsu damage multiplier | 1.0× (baseline) | 1.44× |
| Abyss clear time (typical) | 90-120s per half | 50-70s per half |
| Viable team comps | National, Hyperbloom, Hypercarry | Hypercarry (optimal), any comp |
| Pull priority vs weapon | Pull C2 before Engulfing Lightning | — |

**Pull Strategy**: C0 → C2 → C3. Engulfing Lightning is third priority. C2 is worth more than R1 Engulfing Lightning by a significant margin (~44% vs ~15% damage increase).

### Engulfing Lightning vs The Catch R5

The Catch R5 is the F2P benchmark. At R5, it provides:
- 45.9% ER (substat)
- 32% Burst DMG (passive)
- 12% Burst Crit Rate (passive)

Engulfing Lightning R1 provides:
- 55.1% ER (substat, ~10% more)
- 30% ER for 12s after Burst (active during her 7s Musou window + 5s buffer)
- ATK = 28% of ER above 100% → at 270% ER: (170 × 0.28) = 47.6% ATK

**Damage Difference**: ~18-22% in favor of Engulfing Lightning. The Catch at R5 is 82% as strong. This means The Catch R5 is perfectly viable for 36-star Abyss clears — C2 with The Catch R5 does more damage than C0 with Engulfing Lightning R1.

## Artifact Set: 4-Piece Emblem of Severed Fate

There is no debate. 4pc Emblem is Raiden's BiS in every team composition (Hypercarry, National, Hyperbloom EM). The 2pc (+20% ER) and 4pc (+Burst DMG = 25% of ER) synergize perfectly with her ER stacking and A4 passive.

The 4pc effect caps at 75% Burst DMG (at 300% ER). Since Raiden typically runs 250-300% ER, she reaches near-maximum value from Emblem.

**Alternative Sets (NOT recommended)**:
- 4pc Thundering Fury: Viable for Aggravate driver Raiden, but Hypercarry Raiden does not trigger reactions (Sara/Kazuha/Bennett provide no reaction triggers during her burst window).
- 2pc Emblem + 2pc ATK%: Loses ~20% damage vs 4pc Emblem.
- 4pc Gilded Dreams / 4pc Flower of Paradise Lost: Only for Hyperbloom trigger Raiden (full EM build, different role).

### Main Stats

| Piece | Main Stat | Notes |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| Sands | ER% | Non-negotiable. Raiden's damage, energy generation, and team utility all scale with ER. |
| Goblet | ATK% or Electro DMG% | ATK% with Engulfing Lightning; Electro DMG% with The Catch (less ATK from weapon). At high investment, difference is ~5%. |
| Circlet | Crit Rate or Crit DMG | Balance for 1:2 ratio. The Catch gives 12% Crit Rate on Burst — factor this into your ratio. |

Crit Rate/Crit DMG > ER% (until 270%) > ATK% > Flat ATK

## Complete Rotation (22-second cycle)

The Hypercarry rotation is extremely tight — Kazuha's VV shred and Sara's ATK buff both have short durations, and Raiden's Musou Shinsetsu must fit within both buff windows.

### Step-by-Step Rotation

| Step | Action | Buff Applied | Duration Remaining |
|------|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| 1 | Raiden E | Eye of Stormy Judgment (+24% Burst DMG to team) | 25s |
| 2 | Kazuha E (tap) → Q | VV Electro shred (10s), A4 Electro DMG% (8s) | ~8-10s |
| 3 | Bennett E → Q | ATK buff (12s), Noblesse ATK% (12s) | ~10-12s |
| 4 | Sara E → Q (or just Q if C2+ to save time) | ATK buff (6s), C6 Crit DMG buff (6s) | ~5-6s |
| 5 | Raiden Q | All buffs active. Musou Shinsetsu: N3C × 3 + N1C | Full burst window |
| 6 | Raiden N3C combo | N3C = 3 normal attacks + Charged Attack. Repeat 3 times. Higher MV than N4/N5 strings. | — |
| 7 | Raiden final N1C | If time remains, squeeze one more CA | — |
| 8 | Battery phase | Bennett E → funnel to Sara/Kazuha | — |
| 9 | Repeat from step 1 | Raiden E cooldown should be ready | — |

### The Raiden N3C Combo Explained

Raiden's optimal Normal Attack string during Musou Shinsetsu is N3C (3 Normal Attacks + Charged Attack):

- N1: 79.6% MV (motion value)
- N2: 74.2% MV
- N3: 92.4% MV
- CA: 134.6% MV + 107.1% MV (two hits)
- Total N3C: 488% MV

Repeat ×3: 1,464% MV per burst window. This is the highest sustained damage combo.

Avoid N4 (slower, lower MV% per frame) and N5 (even slower). N1C spam is technically higher DPS but requires C1 (Charged Attacks cost less stamina during burst) to sustain — and loses Resolve hit count.

### Timing Is Everything

| Buff | Source | Duration | Must Cover |
|------|--------|----------|------------|
| VV Electro Shred | Kazuha (Swirl) | 10s | Raiden's entire burst |
| A4 DMG% Bonus | Kazuha | 8s | Raiden's entire burst |
| ATK Buff | Bennett Q | 12s | Raiden's entire burst |
| ATK Buff + Crit DMG | Sara E/Q + C6 | 6s | Raiden's entire 7s burst + initial slash |

Because Sara's buff lasts only 6 seconds and Raiden's burst lasts 7 seconds (including initial slash animation), you have approximately **1 second of buffer**. Swapping from Sara to Raiden and pressing Q must be instant.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Not funneling particles to Sara**: Sara's 80-cost burst is difficult to recharge. After Raiden's burst ends, use Bennett E twice and funnel both particles to Sara before restarting rotation. Raiden's Energy restoration happens during her burst — by the time you're funneling, Sara should have ~50% Energy already if you built 180%+ ER.

2. **Using Kazuha Q too early**: Kazuha's burst animation is long. If you Q before Bennett Q, VV shred wastes 2 seconds during setup. Always Bennett Q → Kazuha Q last before Sara.

3. **ATK% Goblet when using The Catch**: The Catch has low Base ATK (510), meaning ATK% provides less value. Electro DMG% Goblet is mathematically superior with The Catch. Only consider ATK% Goblet with Engulfing Lightning (608 Base ATK + ATK conversion passive).

4. **Building EM on Hypercarry Raiden**: In Hypercarry, Raiden does not trigger reactions. The team (Sara/Kazuha/Bennett) provides no consistent Hydro, Cryo, or Dendro application during Raiden's burst window. All EM substats are dead stats. EM Raiden is a separate build for Hyperbloom teams.
