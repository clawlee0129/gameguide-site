## Introduction

Elden Ring's Platinum trophy, **"Elden Ring"**, requires collecting all 42 achievements across three playthroughs (or one playthrough with save-scumming for the three ending achievements). This guide provides a structured, step-by-step walkthrough to earn every achievement, minimize missed content, and avoid common pitfalls that force extra playthroughs.

**Total Playthroughs Required**: 3 (or 1 with save-scumming)
**Estimated Time**: 80-120 hours
**Missable Achievements**: 4 (see Missable Warning section)
**Difficulty**: 7/10 (patience-testing more than skill-demanding)

## Achievement Categories

## Missable Warning: Critical Path-Locking Items

These 4 achievements can be permanently missed in a playthrough:

### 1. Bolt of Gransax (Legendary Armament)
**Location**: Leyndell, Royal Capital — on the giant spear statue near the Erdtree Sanctuary Grace. **Becomes inaccessible after defeating Maliketh in Crumbling Farum Azula** (Leyndell turns to Ashen Capital). Grab this before completing Farum Azula.

### 2. Devourer's Scepter (Legendary Armament)
**Multiple acquisition methods**:
- Kill **Recusant Bernahl** when he invades you in Farum Azula (near the Beside the Great Bridge Grace)
- OR kill him earlier at Warmaster's Shack (but this locks you out of his questline)
- He drops the scepter in either case
- **Missable if you complete Farum Azula without killing his invasion or him**

### 3. Magic Scorpion Charm (Legendary Talisman)
Gained only by progressing **Seluvis's questline** (Ranni's questline sub-path). Give the Amber Starlight (Altus Plateau) to Seluvis before giving Ranni the Fingerslayer Blade. **If you give Ranni the Fingerslayer Blade first, Seluvis dies and the charm is permanently missable.** This requires either careful quest ordering or NG+.

### 4. Frenzied Flame Ending (Lord of Frenzied Flame)
Once you inherit the **Frenzied Flame** (Three Fingers door, Subterranean Shunning-Grounds), you are locked into the Frenzied Flame ending unless you use **Miquella's Needle** in Placidusax's arena. This needle also requires completing Millicent's questline (challenging). Plan your ending order carefully.

## Playthrough 1: 100% Collection (All Legendary Items + Most Bosses)

This is your main playthrough where you collect everything. Follow the route below to minimize backtracking.

### Phase 1: Limgrave & Weeping Peninsula (Level 1-30)

**Priority**: Get Torrent, Spirit Calling Bell, Whetstone Knife, Roundtable Hold access

1. **Church of Elleh**: Buy Torch, Crafting Kit (Kale). Rest at night — Renna gives **Spirit Calling Bell + Lone Wolf Ashes**.
2. **Gatefront Ruins**: Grab the Map Fragment from the soldier camp. Grab the **Whetstone Knife** from the underground chest.
3. **Rest at Gatefront Grace**: Melina takes you to Roundtable Hold. Achievement: **Roundtable Hold** unlocks.
4. **Fringefolk Hero's Grave** (Stonesword Key required): Navigate the chariot trap. At the end: **Erdtree's Favor** talisman. Achievement: **Erdtree's Favor**.
5. **Murkwater Cave**: Defeat **Patches** (don't kill him, spare him for quest). Buy **Margit's Shackle** (5,000 runes).
6. **Castle Morne** (Weeping Peninsula south): Defeat **Leonine Misbegotten** at the beach behind the castle. Achievement: **Leonine Misbegotten**.
7. **Stormveil Castle**: Defeat **Margit** (Ach: **Margit, the Fell Omen**), then **Godrick the Grafted** (Ach: **Godrick the Grafted**).
8. **Divine Tower of Limgrave**: Activate Godrick's Great Rune. Achievement: **Great Rune** (if you use a Rune Arc and activate — actually, you just need to activate at the tower to get the achievement).

### Phase 2: Liurnia of the Lakes (Level 30-60)

9. **Raya Lucaria Academy**: Defeat **Red Wolf of Radagon** (Ach: **Red Wolf of Radagon**) and **Rennala** (Ach: **Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon**).
10. **Caria Manor**: Defeat **Royal Knight Loretta** (Ach: **Royal Knight Loretta**).
11. **Ranni's Rise** (behind Caria Manor): Speak to Ranni, join her service. Speak to Seluvis, Iji, and Blaidd downstairs.
12. **Seluvis's Quest (critical missable)**: Go to Seluvis's tower. He gives you a potion. Find the **Amber Starlight** in Altus Plateau (in front of a statue near the road). Give it to Seluvis. He rewards you with **Magic Scorpion Charm**. Achievement: **Legendary Talismans** (partial — get this before giving Ranni the Fingerslayer Blade).

### Phase 3: Caelid & Underground (Level 60-80)

13. **Siofra River**: Light all 8 obelisks to summon the Ancestor Spirit. Defeat **Ancestor Spirit** (Ach: **Ancestor Spirit**).
14. **Redmane Castle**: Defeat **Starscourge Radahn** (Ach: **Starscourge Radahn**).
15. **Nokron, Eternal City** (meteor opens access): Grab the **Fingerslayer Blade**. DO NOT give it to Ranni yet (protects Seluvis). Instead:
16. **Mimic Tear boss**: Defeat **Mimic Tear** (Ach: **Mimic Tear**). Grab the **Mimic Tear Ashes** — you'll want this for the rest of the game. Also grab the **Black Whetblade** nearby.
17. **Siofra Aqueduct**: Defeat **Valiant Gargoyles** (Ach: **Valiant Gargoyles**).
18. **Nokron, Night's Sacred Ground**: Grab the **Fingerslayer Blade** from the chest under the giant skeleton statue.
19. Give Fingerslayer Blade to Ranni. This progresses her quest (and kills Seluvis, so make sure you have Magic Scorpion Charm first).

### Phase 4: Altus Plateau & Volcano Manor (Level 80-100)

20. **Ruin-Strewn Precipice** (alt route to Altus): Defeat **Magma Wyrm Makar** (Ach: **Magma Wyrm Makar**).
21. **Shaded Castle**: Defeat **Elemer of the Briar** (Ach: **Elemer of the Briar**). Grab the **Marais Executioner's Sword** and the Valkyrie's Prosthesis (for Millicent quest).
22. **Volcano Manor**: Join Volcano Manor (Tanith). Do the two assassination contracts. Then progress through the dungeon path:
   - Defeat **Godskin Noble** (Ach: **Godskin Noble**)
   - Defeat **Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy** (Ach: **Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy**)

### Phase 5: Leyndell, Royal Capital (Level 100-110)

23. Enter Leyndell through the main gate. Defeat **Draconic Tree Sentinel** to gain access.
24. Defeat **Godfrey, First Elden Lord (Golden Shade)** (Ach: **Godfrey, First Elden Lord**).
25. **CRITICAL — BEFORE GOING FURTHER**: Grab the **Bolt of Gransax** from the giant spear statue near Erdtree Sanctuary. This is the most commonly missed legendary item. Achievement: **Legendary Armaments** (partial).
26. Continue to the Erdtree. Defeat **Morgott, the Omen King** (Ach: **Morgott, the Omen King**).
27. **Subterranean Shunning-Grounds**: Enter the sewers (well near Avenue Balcony Grace). Navigate through the pipes to find **Mohg, the Omen** (NOT Mohg, Lord of Blood). Defeat him (Ach: **Mohg, the Omen**). Behind his arena: the Frenzied Flame Proscription door (don't enter — save for last playthrough).

### Phase 6: Mountaintops & Consecrated Snowfield (Level 110-125)

28. **Mountaintops of the Giants**: Defeat **Fire Giant** (Ach: **Fire Giant**).
29. **Castle Sol**: Defeat **Commander Niall** (Ach: **Commander Niall**). Grab the Haligtree Secret Medallion (Left) from the roof.
30. **Consecrated Snowfield**: Use both Haligtree Medallion halves at the Grand Lift of Rold. Navigate through the blizzard. Light the torches in the **Ordina, Liturgical Town** evergaol to unlock the Haligtree portal.

### Phase 7: Miquella's Haligtree & Elphael (Level 125-140)

31. **Miquella's Haligtree**: Navigate the branches. Defeat **Loretta, Knight of the Haligtree** (Ach: **Loretta, Knight of the Haligtree**).
32. **Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree**: Push through to the bottom.
33. Defeat **Malenia, Blade of Miquella** (Ach: **Malenia, Blade of Miquella**). This is the hardest boss in the base game. Use the Bleed Build guide for an easier time.

### Phase 8: Endgame Cleanup (Level 140+)

34. **Deeproot Depths**: After defeating Valiant Gargoyles, take the coffin in their arena. Follow Fia's questline. At the end: **Lichdragon Fortissax** (Ach: **Lichdragon Fortissax**).
35. **Lake of Rot**: Navigate to the Grand Cloister. Defeat **Astel, Naturalborn of the Void** (Ach: **Astel, Naturalborn of the Void**).
36. **Mohgwyn Palace**: Use the Pureblood Knight's Medal (Varre quest) or the Consecrated Snowfield portal. Defeat **Mohg, Lord of Blood** (Ach: **Shardbearer Mohg**).
37. **Crumbling Farum Azula**: Defeat **Godskin Duo** (Ach: **Godskin Duo**). Find the hidden path to **Dragonlord Placidusax** (lie down on the platform near the tornado). Defeat him (Ach: **Dragonlord Placidusax**). Defeat **Maliketh, the Black Blade** (Ach: **Maliketh, the Black Blade**).

**NOTE**: After Maliketh, Leyndell becomes Ashen Capital. The Bolt of Gransax is now permanently inaccessible. Confirm Legendary Armaments is complete before this point.

## Playthrough 2: Elden Lord Ending (Speedrun)

After completing Playthrough 1 with all legendary items, start NG+. Your gear, levels, and upgrade materials carry over.

**Goal**: Elden Lord ending (any variant — Age of Duskborn, Age of Order, Age of Despair, or standard).

**Route**: Speedrun to the end. Skip all optional content. Kill Margit → Godrick → Rennala → Draconic Tree Sentinel → Golden Godfrey → Morgott → Fire Giant → Godskin Duo → Maliketh → Hoarah Loux → Radagon/Elden Beast. At the end, choose "Mend the Elden Ring" (or any variant — all count as Elden Lord).

Estimated time: 2-3 hours for a focused NG+ run.

Achievement: **Elden Lord**.

## Playthrough 3: Frenzied Flame + Age of Stars (or vice versa, with save-scum)

### Option A: Two More Full Playthroughs

Do a third NG++ run for **Age of the Stars** (complete Ranni's quest entirely) and a fourth for **Lord of Frenzied Flame** (inherit Frenzied Flame before final boss).

### Option B: Save-Scumming (Recommended)

On Playthrough 2, after defeating the final boss but before interacting with the Fractured Marika, manually backup your save:

**Save file location**: `C:\Users\[Username]\AppData\Roaming\EldenRing\[SteamID]\`

1. After Radagon/Elden Beast are dead, quit the game
2. Backup the save folder
3. Launch game, choose ending #1 (e.g., Age of Stars — summon Ranni)
4. Achievement pops. Quit. Restore save.
5. Launch game, choose ending #2 (Elden Lord)
6. Achievement pops. Quit. Restore save.
7. Launch game, choose ending #3 (Lord of Frenzied Flame — must have inherited Frenzied Flame first)

This method requires completing **Ranni's entire questline** (Age of Stars) and inheriting the **Frenzied Flame** (Three Fingers door) before the final boss fight in the same playthrough.

### Frenzied Flame Route Quick Guide

1. Reach the **Subterranean Shunning-Grounds** (sewer well near Avenue Balcony Grace, Leyndell)
2. Navigate past Mohg, the Omen boss
3. Behind Mohg's arena: a hidden altar. Hit the altar to reveal a path to the **Three Fingers** door
4. Remove ALL armor (must be naked) to open the door
5. Inherit the Frenzied Flame (permanent until cured with Miquella's Needle)
6. Now all three endings are available in one playthrough

## Final Tips

1. **Backup your save before Malekith**. This is the single most important checkpoint. If you miss the Bolt of Gransax, you can revert.
2. **Do Ranni's questline thoroughly**. It unlocks 3 legendary items (Dark Moon Greatsword, Ranni's Dark Moon, Black Knife Tiche).
3. **The Magic Scorpion Charm from Seluvis is the easiest legendary to miss**. Do his Amber Starlight task before giving Ranni the Fingerslayer Blade.
4. **Use the Bleed build for Malenia**. She's the hardest roadblock. Corpse Piler (Rivers of Blood) plus Mimic Tear +10 makes her manageable.
5. **Keep a checklist**. The game has no in-game achievement tracker for collectibles. Use the checklist above.
