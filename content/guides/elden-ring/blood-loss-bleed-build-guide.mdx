## Introduction

Bleed (Hemorrhage) is arguably the most powerful status effect in Elden Ring. Each bleed proc deals percentage-based damage — approximately **15% of the target's maximum HP** plus a flat 100-200 damage depending on your weapon — making it devastating against bosses with massive health pools. This guide covers the optimal Bleed build at every stage of the game, from the moment you step into Limgrave to endgame Malenia-slaying setups.

## How Bleed Works

**Bleed Buildup** is applied with each hit from a weapon that has innate Bleed or is infused with the Blood affinity. When the invisible Bleed meter fills, the target takes:
- **15% of max HP** (bosses) plus flat damage
- **30% of max HP** (standard enemies)
- A brief stagger animation

Higher Arcane increases Bleed buildup IF the weapon has Arcane scaling. At 45 Arcane, bleed buildup values roughly double compared to base Arcane.

**Bleed-Immune enemies**: Radagon, Elden Beast, Valiant Gargoyles, Erdtree Avatars, Putrid Avatars, Burial Watchdogs, Skeletons, Spirit-Caller Snails, Crystallians. Against these, fall back on pure physical damage.

### Dual Scavenger's Curved Swords (Highest Bleed DPS)

At 50 Arcane with Blood affinity, each curved sword applies ~85 Bleed per hit. Power-stance jumping L1 hits 4 times, applying 340 Bleed instantly. Combined with the curved sword's fast running attacks and the multi-hit nature of jump L1s, this setup has the highest Bleed application rate in the game.

## Combat Strategy & Rotations

### Pre-Fight Buff Sequence

1. Switch to offhand **Finger Seal**
2. Cast **Golden Vow** (if 25 Faith) or **Flame, Grant Me Strength**
3. Apply **Bloodflame Blade** (only on non-Somber weapons)
4. Drink **Wondrous Physick**
5. Switch to dual katana power-stance
6. Enter fog gate

### In-Fight Priority

1. **Opening**: Jump L1 (4 hits with dual curved swords). Applies 300+ Bleed.
2. **Spam**: Corpse Piler L2. Each cast hits 6+ times. After 2-3 casts, Bleed procs.
3. **On Bleed Proc**: Boss staggers briefly. Use this window for a charged R2 or another Corpse Piler.
4. **When FP is low**: Switch to L1 combos. Jump L1 deals excellent poise damage.
5. **Against Bleed-immune bosses**: Respect the limitations. Corpse Piler still deals fire/physical damage — it's just slower. Use jump attacks for poise breaks.

### Ash of War: Seppuku

If using non-Somber weapons, **Seppuku** (Ash of War from frozen lake, Mountaintops of the Giants) can be equipped on your offhand weapon. Using it costs 50 HP and applies +30 Bleed buildup and +30 physical damage for 60 seconds. The self-damage also triggers Lord of Blood's Exultation and White Mask, giving you +30% AR before the fight starts.

**Optimal Seppuku setup**: Blood Uchigatana +25 with Seppuku. Use it, then switch to Rivers of Blood. The Seppuku buff persists on the offhand weapon while you Corpse Piler with the main hand.

## Boss-Specific Tips

| Boss | Strategy |
|------|----------|
| **Malenia** | Corpse Piler spam. She staggers from every hit. Two casts + bleed proc deletes 30% HP. Mimic Tear with the same setup doubles bleed output. |
| **Mohg** | Bleed on the blood lord! Proc bleed 3-4 times during NIHIL channel. He's ironically one of the easiest Remembrance bosses for bleed builds. |
| **Fire Giant** | Target the wrapped ankle. Bleed procs for ~15% of his massive HP bar = 3,000+ damage per proc. |
| **Godskin Duo** | Bleed works on both. Proc bleed separately on each. Sleep one with pots, bleed the other. |
| **Maliketh** | Phase 1 bleed is very effective. Phase 2 Maliketh is highly mobile — bring frost pots to slow him. |
| **Radagon** | IMMUNE to bleed. Fall back on pure physical Corpse Piler damage. Switch to Occult affinity if possible. |
| **Elden Beast** | IMMUNE. Use Black Flame Tornado or Pest Threads instead. |
