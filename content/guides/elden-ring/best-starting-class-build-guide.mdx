## Introduction

Choosing the right build in Elden Ring can mean the difference between struggling through every encounter and confidently conquering the Lands Between. This guide covers five optimal starting builds, each designed to carry you from Limgrave to the Elden Throne. Every build includes specific stat allocations at key level milestones, recommended weapons with upgrade paths, talisman setups, Flask distributions, and combat strategies.

Builds scale with your playthrough. We provide stat targets at Level 50 (early game), Level 100 (mid game), and Level 150 (end game / PvP meta), so you always know where to invest your next Rune.

**Best Starting Class**: Vagabond  
**Playstyle**: Balanced melee using both Strength and Dexterity weapons with heavy armor and medium roll.

### Combat Strategy

The Quality build excels with Jump Attacks — equip the **Claw Talisman** for +15% jump attack damage and the **Raptor's Black Feathers** chest armor for another +10%. Power-stance two weapons of the same type for the L1 combo, which deals massive poise damage. Use **Flame of the Redmanes** Ash of War (dropped by invisible scarab near Fort Gael North) for 40 poise damage per cast — two casts stance-break most bosses.

## Build 3: The Samurai Bleed Build

**Best Starting Class**: Samurai (or Bandit for pure Arcane)  
**Playstyle**: Fast-hitting dual-wield or two-handed with Bleed procs dominating boss fights.

### Stat Progression

| Level | Vigor | Mind | Endurance | Strength | Dexterity | Intelligence | Faith | Arcane |
|-------|-------|------|----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-------|--------|
| 50 | 25 | 12 | 15 | 12 | 30 | 9 | 8 | 20 |
| 100 | 40 | 15 | 20 | 12 | 45 | 9 | 8 | 35 |
| 150 | 50 | 15 | 25 | 12 | 55 | 9 | 8 | 45 |

Arcane increases Bleed build-up rate when the weapon has Arcane scaling. At 45 Arcane with a Blood-infused Uchigatana, you proc Bleed in 3–4 hits against most bosses.

### Weapon Progression

| Stage | Weapon | Acquisition | Blood Loss |
|-------|--------|-------------|------------|
| Early | **Uchigatana** x2 | Starting weapon + Death-Touched Catacombs | 45 per hit (each) |
| Early | **Reduvia** | Nerijus invasion, Agheel Lake | 50 per hit |
| Mid | **Eleonora's Poleblade** | Bloody Finger Eleonora, Altus | 55 per hit, twinblade |
| Mid | **Godskin Peeler** | Godskin Apostle, Windmill Village | 45 per hit, twinblade |
| Late | **Rivers of Blood** | Bloody Finger Okina, Mountaintops | 55 per hit, Corpse Piler WA |
| Endgame | **Morgott's Cursed Sword** | Morgott Remembrance | 60 per hit |

### Optimal Dual-Wield Setup (Level 150)

**Main hand**: **Rivers of Blood +10** (Somber)  
**Off hand**: **Blood Uchigatana +25** (Blood affinity, 85 blood loss buildup at 45 Arcane)

With this setup, Corpse Piler weapon art from Rivers of Blood applies massive Bleed while the L1 dual-wield combo procs additional procs. Against Malenia, you can proc Bleed every 4–5 seconds.

### Talismans

| Slot | Talisman | Effect |
|------|----------|--------|
| 1 | **Lord of Blood's Exultation** | +20% AR for 20s after Bleed proc nearby |
| 2 | **Rotten Winged Sword Insignia** | +6%/8%/13% damage on successive hits (stacks 3x) |
| 3 | **Millicent's Prosthesis** | +5 Dex, +4%/6%/11% successive attack boost |
| 4 | **Shard of Alexander** | +15% weapon skill damage |

With 4 successive hits, the stacked talismans provide +24% damage (RWSI + Millicent's), and Corpse Piler hits 6+ times instantly.

### White Mask + Raptor's Black Feathers Armor

**White Mask** (dropped by Nameless White Mask invaders in Mohgwyn Palace): +10% AR for 20s when ANY Bleed procs nearby. **Raptor's Black Feathers** (Sage's Cave, Altus): +10% jump attack damage. Combined setup: Bleed proc triggers White Mask + Lord of Blood's Exultation for +30% total AR, then jump attack with dual katanas.

### Flask Distribution

- **Level 50**: 6 Crimson / 2 Cerulean / 1 Wondrous
- **Level 100**: 8 Crimson / 3 Cerulean
- **Level 150**: 10 Crimson / 2 Cerulean (Corpse Piler is FP-efficient)

### Combat Strategy

For bosses that bleed: Open with Seppuku (Ash of War from frozen lake in Mountaintops) on offhand Uchigatana for instant Bleed proc and White Mask activation, then Corpse Piler spam. Against bleed-immune bosses (Radagon, Elden Beast, Valiant Gargoyles, Erdtree Avatars), switch to **Occult** affinity — your Arcane scaling provides pure physical damage without relying on Bleed.

## General Build Tips

1. **Vigor is your most important stat until 40**. Survivability trumps damage in Elden Ring. Every build should hit 40 Vigor by Level 80 and 50–60 by endgame.

2. **Endurance determines your equip load**. Medium roll requires <70% equip load. Light roll (<30%) gives more iframes but requires heavy Endurance investment.

3. **Mind** only needs 20–25 for pure melee builds (enough for weapon arts and spirit summons). Caster builds want 30–38.

4. **Softcaps matter**: Vigor softcaps at 40 (1450 HP) and 60 (1900 HP). Damage stats softcap at 20, 55, and 80 (Int/Faith/Arcane scale well to 80; Str/Dex to 55 for Quality).

5. Always keep a strike weapon in your inventory — Crystallians and miners are nearly immune to slash/pierce but stagger easily from strike damage.
